Privacy Policy for Weekora

Weekora is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and delete your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Such as your email address when you register or use the app.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app (e.g., features used, actions taken).
  • Device Information: Basic technical data such as device type, OS version, and app version for performance and debugging.
  • Images (Camera Data): Only when you actively use the camera feature for scanning food or capturing images.

Permissions We Use

  • Camera: Used to scan food or capture images for app functionality.
  • Internet Access: Required to connect with our servers and deliver features.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate app features
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Communicate important updates (if applicable)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal obligations
  • To protect our rights, safety, and users
  • With trusted service providers (if any) who help operate the app, under strict confidentiality

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill legal obligations. If your account remains active, your data will be stored securely. Inactive or unused data may be periodically deleted or anonymized.

Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. Upon receiving a valid request, we will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days, unless retention is required by law.

Account Deletion Request

To delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at:

Email: Support@weekora.ai

Please include your registered email address in your request.

Children’s Privacy

Weekora does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on our official page.
